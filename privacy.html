Privacy notice

This page is not ready to serve as a public policy or App Store privacy URL. It is prepared for intended publisher AI EMPIRE INC. (Ontario, Canada). A monitored privacy contact, effective date, public URL, and professional review are still required. Confirm every statement against the final signed app.

Information stored on this iPhone

When you choose to save them, notes, manually recorded event entries, their correction history, and appointment-selection state are stored in PD Bridge’s encrypted local vault. Optional voice recordings begin only after you tap Start and are saved only after review. PD Bridge does not transcribe audio. Temporary recording files are protected, removed after review, and excluded from backup.

The vault uses AES-GCM encryption. Its key is kept in this iPhone’s device-only Keychain and is available only while the iPhone is unlocked. Vault files use iOS file protection and are excluded from iPhone and iCloud device backups. If your iPhone is lost, erased, or the app is removed and you do not have a usable encrypted export, your diary may be unrecoverable.

App preferences such as interface language, Comfort Mode, and the generic reminder time are stored separately in iOS app settings. They do not contain observation text, medication names, or doses. iOS may include ordinary app preferences in a device backup. PD Bridge does not synchronize preferences through iCloud key-value storage.

Network and sharing

The inspected V1 app source does not send diary entries, audio, reminder preferences, caregiver details, or identifiers to a PD Bridge server, analytics, advertising, transcription, or external AI service. The app has no PD Bridge account or hosted diary. Optional reminders are scheduled locally by iOS; delivery may be delayed or missed because of permission, Focus, device, or system settings.

If you create an encrypted backup, PD Bridge encrypts it with a passphrase you provide. The app does not keep that passphrase. You choose the Files location. A cloud-backed provider may sync the encrypted file under its own terms. PD Bridge cannot access or delete that exported copy. Encrypted exports include saved voice notes but not app preferences or reminder settings.

In Help, you may type a caregiver’s name and telephone number. These values are temporary, are not saved in the diary, and are cleared when you cancel or leave the flow. PD Bridge opens Phone only after you choose Open Phone. It does not call automatically or share your location.

Retention and deletion

Saved local entries remain until you delete them, delete the local diary, remove the app, or the device is lost or erased. Delete an entry in History; Undo is available only while its in-app action remains available. Delete all local diary entries from Settings. Deletion removes the local key and ciphertext and keeps a device-only marker to reject older queued test events. It does not guarantee physical overwriting of flash storage and cannot delete encrypted exports saved elsewhere. Delete external backup files separately through Files or the provider you chose.

There is no PD Bridge server-side copy or server retention period in this V1 flow. The developer cannot recover a forgotten export passphrase.

Purpose and limits

PD Bridge is intended to help a person record observations they choose to keep and organize selected entries for discussion at an appointment. It does not diagnose, assess disease severity or progression, predict falls, recommend treatment or medication changes, determine medical causation, monitor emergencies, or replace care from a qualified professional. It is not an emergency-response service.
