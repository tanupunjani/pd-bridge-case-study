Privacy Policy

AI EMPIRE INC. (Ontario, Canada) provides this policy for the PD Bridge iPhone app. It describes the current V1 app and this support website. We will update it if the app's data handling changes.

Information stored on this iPhone

When you choose to save them, notes, manually recorded event entries, their correction history, and appointment-selection state are stored in PD Bridge’s encrypted local vault. Optional voice recordings begin only after you tap Start and are saved only after review. PD Bridge does not transcribe audio. Temporary recording files use iOS file protection and are excluded from backup. The app attempts to remove them after review and to clean up abandoned files on a later app start.

The vault uses AES-GCM encryption. Its key is kept in this iPhone’s device-only Keychain and is available only while the iPhone is unlocked. Vault files use iOS file protection and are excluded from iPhone and iCloud device backups. If your iPhone is lost, erased, or the app is removed and you do not have a usable encrypted export, your diary may be unrecoverable.

App preferences such as interface language, Comfort Mode, and the generic reminder time are stored separately in iOS app settings. They do not contain observation text, medication names, or doses. iOS may include ordinary app preferences in a device backup. PD Bridge does not synchronize preferences through iCloud key-value storage.

Network and sharing

The inspected V1 app source does not send diary entries, audio, reminder preferences, caregiver details, or identifiers to a PD Bridge server, analytics, advertising, transcription, or external AI service. The app has no PD Bridge account or hosted diary. If you open the Privacy Policy link in Settings, your browser requests this GitHub Pages website; GitHub may receive ordinary web request information such as your IP address and browser details. This website also requests display fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive ordinary web request information. The app does not add diary content to those requests. Optional reminders are scheduled locally by iOS; delivery may be delayed or missed because of permission, Focus, device, or system settings.

If you create an encrypted backup, PD Bridge encrypts it with a passphrase you provide. The app does not keep that passphrase. You choose the Files location. A cloud-backed provider may sync the encrypted file under its own terms. PD Bridge cannot access or delete that exported copy. Encrypted exports include saved voice notes but not app preferences or reminder settings.

In Help, you may type a caregiver’s name and telephone number. These values are temporary, are not saved in the diary, and are cleared when you cancel or leave the flow. PD Bridge opens Phone only after you choose Open Phone. It does not call automatically or share your location.

Retention and deletion

Saved local entries remain until you delete them, delete the local diary, remove the app, or the device is lost or erased. Delete an entry in History; Undo is available only while its in-app action remains available. Delete all local diary entries from Settings. Deletion removes the local key and ciphertext and keeps a device-only marker to reject older queued test events. If protected storage is unavailable or cleanup fails, the app reports that deletion is incomplete and retries when reopened. Deletion does not guarantee physical overwriting of flash storage and cannot delete encrypted exports saved elsewhere. Delete external backup files separately through Files or the provider you chose. Importing an older encrypted export can restore entries that were previously deleted.

There is no PD Bridge server-side copy or server retention period in this V1 flow. The developer cannot recover a forgotten export passphrase.

Purpose and limits

PD Bridge is intended to help a person record observations they choose to keep and organize selected entries for discussion at an appointment. It does not diagnose, assess disease severity or progression, predict falls, recommend treatment or medication changes, determine medical causation, monitor emergencies, or replace care from a qualified professional. It is not an emergency-response service.

PD Bridge has no hosted diary for us to access; you can review or delete local entries in the app. We monitor this inbox but do not promise a particular response time.
