PD Bridge support

AI EMPIRE INC. (Ontario, Canada) provides this support information for PD Bridge.

PD Bridge is an observation journal and appointment-preparation tool. It is not medical advice, automatic fall detection, emergency monitoring, or an emergency-response service. For urgent help, contact local emergency services directly.

Protect your information

Saved observations and optional voice recordings are stored in an encrypted on-device vault. The vault files are excluded from iPhone and iCloud device backups. App preferences such as language, Comfort Mode, and the generic reminder schedule are stored separately and may be included in a device backup. There is no PD Bridge cloud account or diary synchronization.

Use Settings to create a passphrase-encrypted export before changing or replacing your iPhone. Keep both the file and passphrase private. If you save it to a cloud Files provider, that provider may sync the encrypted file. PD Bridge cannot delete copies saved outside the app.

Export, restore, and delete

  • Export: Settings → Encrypted backup → Export, confirm, then choose a private Files destination. Voice-note audio is included; app preferences and reminder settings are not.
  • Restore: Settings → Encrypted backup → Import, select the encrypted file, review the merge warning, and enter its passphrase. Importing an older backup can restore entries previously deleted from the current diary.
  • Delete one entry: History → entry → Delete. Use Undo only while the in-app action remains available.
  • Delete the local diary: Settings → Delete all observations and confirm. Separately delete exported files from Files or the provider you chose.

Local deletion does not guarantee physical overwriting of flash storage and cannot remove exported copies. If the device is lost and no usable encrypted export exists, local records may be unrecoverable. PD Bridge cannot recover a forgotten export passphrase.

Microphone, reminders, and caregiver Help

Voice recording is optional, starts only after you tap Start, and saves after review. The original audio is not transcribed or uploaded. The generic optional reminder is scheduled locally; iOS may delay or suppress delivery. The caregiver Help flow holds name and telephone number temporarily. It opens Phone only after you tap Open Phone; it does not make an automatic call or share location.
